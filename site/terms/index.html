Swear-word families

The five most-used swear-word families in member-attributed House and Senate text. Variants are combined; annual rates use each party's attributed word total.

By Hans W. A. Hanley

Updated through October 15, 2026

Sources and how to read these graphs

Lexical matches in published member-attributed text, not verified personally uttered curses. Quotation-mark masking and inserted-material detection are imperfect; literal uses and quoted or printed material can still match.

These graphs use GovInfo member-level data, not the separate Stanford series back to 1873.

Included word families

Included forms: da*n, da***d, da***t, go****n, go******d, go******t, go******t, g*d da*n, g*d da***d, g*d da***t, g*d damnit.

Recognized quotations and inserted material are excluded. Masked source spellings are not inferred. Zero means no accepted matches, not that a word is missing from the codebook.
