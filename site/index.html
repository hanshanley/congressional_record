How this is measured — and what it does not mean

  • Counts come from a deliberately narrow, hand-curated profanity list, not a broad word list, so ordinary words are never counted as swearing.
  • Passages the Record marks as quotations are excluded — a member reading someone else's words is not swearing. Those hits are counted separately and shown below.
  • Only remarks attributable to a specific member by Bioguide ID are counted. Procedural speech by the Chair and presiding officers is excluded.
  • Members below the word threshold are omitted entirely: a rate computed on a few hundred words of floor time is noise, not a ranking.
  • The Congressional Record is a lightly edited transcript. It is not a verbatim record, and members may revise remarks.
